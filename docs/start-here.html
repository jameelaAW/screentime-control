Get started in 5 steps

  1. Open the app link on your phone or computer.
  2. Tap Create an account. Enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
  3. Confirm your email. We send you a message with a link. Open it. If it does not arrive, check your Spam or Junk folder.
  4. Go back to the app and sign in with the same email and password.
  5. When asked, enter your licence key and press Activate. You only do this once.
Where is my licence key? It is on your Payhip receipt and in the email Payhip sent you after your purchase. One key works for one account. Keep it private.

Then use it

  1. Open Children & limits, press + Add child, and set a name and a daily limit in minutes.
  2. On Today's dashboard, press Log in / start on the child's card when they begin a screen.
  3. Press Log out / stop when they finish. The bar turns green, amber, then red as they near the limit.

Put it on your phone

iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

Good to know

  • It tracks time. It cannot see or block a child's device.
  • The timer stops itself at the daily limit, and after 3 hours if you forget.
  • Reminders show only while the app is open.
