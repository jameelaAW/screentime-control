Know how long your children have been on screens, and stop arguing about it.

Screen Time Control is a simple web app for parents and carers. Add your children, press Start when a screen goes on and Stop when it comes off. The app keeps the count against each child's daily limit.

WHAT YOU GET

- A profile for each child with their own daily limit
- A timer for each screen session: TV, tablet, phone, computer or game console, with the activity (video, game, educational and so on)
- Today's dashboard showing time used and time left for each child
- A warning shortly before the limit and an alert when it is reached
- Phone alerts that arrive even when the app is closed
- The timer stops itself at the limit, and a forgotten timer is stopped after 3 hours
- Shared use: parents or carers sign in with the same email and password and see the same data on their own phones
- A readable report of your data you can save as a PDF, plus a spreadsheet (CSV) download
- A built-in user guide and a one-page Start-here guide
- Delete your account and data yourself at any time

HOW IT WORKS

1. Pay first. Buy on Payhip. Your licence key arrives straight away, on the receipt and by email.
2. Create your account. Open the app link and sign up with the email you want to keep. Confirm it from the email we send.
3. Activate. Sign in and paste your licence key when asked. You only do this once. One key works for one account.
4. Start. Add your children, set their daily limits and start timing.

Tip: keep your Payhip receipt. If you ever lose the key, it is there.

It works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. You can add it to your Home Screen so it opens like an app.

- This is a tracker and reminder tool. It does not lock or block a child's device. You press Start and Stop, and the alerts tell you when time is up.
